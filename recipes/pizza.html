Pizza

Recipe

Pizza is a savory Italian dish featuring a flattened leavened wheat dough base, classically layered with a flavourdul tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various toppings, then baked at high temperatures.

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cups warm water
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 2¼ teaspoons active dry or instant yeast
  • ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened at room temperature
  • 1 cup granulated white sugar
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
