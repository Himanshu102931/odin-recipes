Butter Chicken

Recipe

Butter Chicken (or Murgh Makhani) is a rich, creamy Indian curry featuring marinated chicken cooked in a spiced tomato and butter sauce

Ingredients

The Chicken Marinade

  • Chicken: 1.5 lbs (about 750 g)
  • Yogurt: 0.5 cup plain yougurt
  • Citrus: 1 to 2 Tbsp fresh lemon
  • Spices: 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt: 0.75 to 1 tsp

The Curry Sauce (Gravy)

  • Base Fats: 2-4 Tbsp butter
  • Tomatoes: 1 cup tomato paste
  • The Cream: 1 cup heavy cream
  • Key Spices: 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp garam masala, and 1 tsp chili powder
  • The Secret Ingredient: 1-2 Tbsp kasuri methi
  • Finishing Touches: 1 tsp sugar or honey

Steps

Step 1: Marinate the Chicken

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
  2. Add the chicken pieces and mix well until completely coated.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or ideally overnight for deeper flavor.

Step 2: Cook the Chicken

  1. Heat 1 tbsp of oil and 1 tbsp of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the marinated chicken in a single layer (do not overcrowd the pan). Cook for 4-5 minutes per side until beautifully charred and fully cooked through.
  3. Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside on a plate.

Step 3: Prepare the Makhani Sauce

  1. In the same skillet, melt another tablespoon of butter. Add the chopped onions and sauté until soft and translucent (about 3-4 minutes).
  2. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and let it cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes, cashews, chili powder, cumin, garam masala, and a pinch of salt. Simmer over medium-low heat for about 10-15 minutes, or until the tomatoes are broken down and the cashews are soft.
  4. Remove from heat and let it cool slightly. Transfer the sauce to a blender and blend until completely smooth and velvety.

Step 4: Bring It All Together

  1. Pour the blended sauce back into the skillet over medium-low heat.
  2. Stir in the heavy cream and sugar. Let it simmer for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Crush the kasuri methi in your palms and add it to the sauce.
  4. Add the cooked chicken back into the sauce. Simmer everything together for 5 minutes so the chicken absorbs the flavors of the gravy.
  5. Taste and adjust salt or sugar if needed. Serve hot with garlic naan or basmati rice.
