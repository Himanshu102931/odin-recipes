Pizza

Recipe

Pizza is a savory Italian dish featuring a flattened leavened wheat dough base, classically layered with a flavourdul tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various toppings, then baked at high temperatures.

Ingredients

The Pizza Dough

  • 1 ¾ cups warm water
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 2¼ teaspoons active dry or instant yeast
  • 3 to 4 cups unbleached all-purpose flour (or bread flour for extra chew)
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Cornmeal (for dusting)

The Pizza Sauce

  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes or tomato puree
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning or dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional, to balance acidity)

Assembly and Baking

  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes or tomato puree
  • Toppings of your choice

Steps

1. Make the Pizza Dough

  1. Activate the yeast: In a large bowl, combine \(1\frac{1}{3}\) cups warm water (100°F - 110°F), 1 tablespoon granulated sugar, and \(2\frac{1}{4}\) teaspoons instant yeast. Let it sit for about 5–10 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. Mix the ingredients: Add \(3\frac{1}{2}\) to 4 cups of all-purpose flour (or bread flour), 2 tablespoons of olive oil, and 1 teaspoon of salt to the yeast mixture. Stir with a wooden spoon until the dough starts to form.
  3. Knead: Turn the dough onto a floured surface and knead for 10–15 minutes by hand (or 5–10 minutes with a stand mixer) until it is smooth and elastic.
  4. Proof: Lightly oil a bowl, place the dough inside, and cover it with a damp towel or plastic wrap. Let it rise in a warm place for 1.5 to 2 hours, or until it doubles in size.
