Cake

Recipe

A cake is a baked sweet dessert made from flour, sugar, eggs, and fat (like butter or oil).

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 ¾ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened at room temperature
  • 1 cup granulated white sugar
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup whole milk, room temperature

Steps

  1. Prep: Preheat your oven to 170° C (340° F) and lightly grease a 20 cm (8-inch) round cake pan.
  2. Whisk Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Mix Wet Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk the eggs and powdered sugar until smooth. Stir in the oil, yogurt (or buttermilk), milk, and vanilla extract.
  4. Combine: Gradually fold the dry flour mixture into the wet ingredients until completely smooth. Do not overmix.
  5. Bake: Pour the batter into your prepared pan and bake for 35 to 40 minutes. The cake is done when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool: Let the cake cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then invert onto a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.
